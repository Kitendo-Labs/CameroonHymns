Cameroon Hymns Christmas Challenge

Sing the hymns again this Christmas. Your grandparents sang them. Your church still sings them. This Christmas, it's your turn.

Invite & Join Leaderboard Official Rules

For 3 weeks before Christmas, sing a Presbyterian or Catholic hymn with the Cameroon Hymns app, post it on TikTok or Facebook, and invite friends to sing with you using your personal code. These are the official rules.

At a glance

  • Hashtags: #CameroonHymnsChallenge #kitendo
  • Platforms: TikTok and Facebook
  • Prizes: 20,000 / 15,000 / 10,000 FCFA, plus a possible 3-month paid content partnership
  • Format: a skill contest judged on the criteria below. It is not a lottery or draw.

Who can participate

Anyone with a public TikTok or Facebook account can enter, as an individual or as a group (a choir, a family, a youth group).

  • Participants under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission; any prize is paid to the guardian.
  • Prizes are paid by Mobile Money to a Cameroonian number in the winner's (or guardian's) name.
  • A group enters as one participant with one code; a group prize is paid to one named representative.
  • Not eligible for prizes: Cameroon Hymns team members and their immediate families, jury members, and creators we pay to post during the launch week.

How to enter

  1. Install or update the Cameroon Hymns app (Android or iPhone).
  2. Open Join the Challenge on the home screen, enter your display name and your TikTok and/or Facebook handle, and accept these rules.
  3. The app gives you a personal invite code (for example NGOH25) and a personal link. Come back to this screen any time to see them and your verified install count.
  4. Record yourself singing a hymn from the app (see Video requirements).
  5. Post it publicly on TikTok or Facebook with #CameroonHymnsChallenge and #kitendo, tag our page, and put your invite code in the caption.
  6. Share your personal link and code so people install the app with your code.

Each participant may post up to 2 entries; only the higher-scoring entry counts. Installs from both entries count, because they share one code.

Video requirements

  • Hymn: taken from the Presbyterian or Catholic hymnal in the app, in any language the app carries. Christmas hymns are especially welcome.
  • The app on screen: the app is clearly visible showing the hymn being sung, at least in the first seconds of the video.
  • Length: 30 seconds to 3 minutes.
  • Your own voice: live singing; instruments and backing singers are welcome. No lip-syncing to a recording.
  • Posted publicly between the opening and closing dates, and kept up until the winners are announced.
  • Caption: #CameroonHymnsChallenge and #kitendo, a tag of our page, and your invite code. The caption the app suggests does not include #kitendo yet, so add it.
  • Respectful: a setting and presentation suitable for worship music. No mockery of the hymns or of any church.

Judging and scoring

Each participant gets a score out of 100. The three highest scores win.

PartPointsHow it is measured
Jury50Voice and musicality (25), presentation and creativity (15), how well the app features in the video (10)
Verified installs30Your verified installs ÷ the highest count of any participant × 30
Engagement20Views + shares + comments on your best entry ÷ the highest of any entry × 20, counted the day after entries close

A verified install is a new app user who joined with your code (through your link or by typing it) and opened the app on at least 2 different days before the install cut-off. Reinstalls on the same phone, emulators and installs we judge to be fake do not count.

  • The jury has 3 members, including at least one church music representative. Each scores every eligible entry independently; scores are averaged.
  • Ties are broken by the jury score.
  • The public leaderboard shows verified installs per code during the contest. Final results are published with each winner's breakdown.
  • The organisers' decisions on eligibility and results are final.

Content partnership

We may offer one standout participant a paid 3-month content partnership. It is chosen separately from the prizes and need not go to the 1st-place winner.

  • Selection: singing, video quality, reliability and fit with the app, across all entries.
  • Work: 3 short-form hymn videos a week, posted on Cameroon Hymns' TikTok and Facebook.
  • Pay: 20,000 FCFA a month for the videos delivered, plus a 5,000 FCFA bonus in any month with 5 videos a week.
  • Term: January to March 2027, under a separate written agreement. Either side may end it with 2 weeks' notice.
  • We may make no offer if no participant fits.

Fair play and disqualification

  • No bought views, likes, followers or comments, and no bots.
  • No fake installs: no install farms, emulators, or installing and reinstalling on the same phones.
  • One registration per person or group. Codes cannot be shared, sold or transferred.
  • No content that attacks or mocks a church, denomination or person.
  • We may remove installs or engagement we judge suspicious, and may disqualify a participant at any time, including after results are announced.

Rights, privacy and general terms

  • Your video: by entering, you allow Cameroon Hymns to repost and feature your entry, credited to you, on our social pages and in app promotion for 12 months. You can ask us to remove it at any time after the contest ends.
  • Data we collect: display name, social handles, invite code, and anonymous app usage linked to your code (installs and days active). Winners' Mobile Money numbers are collected only for payment.
  • Use of data: only to run the contest and pay prizes; never sold or shared. Registration data is deleted 6 months after the contest ends, except what we must keep for payment records.
  • Platforms: this contest is not sponsored, endorsed or run by TikTok or Meta (Facebook).
  • Changes: we may change dates or rules if needed and will announce any change on our pages. If the contest cannot run fairly, we may cancel it.
  • Contact: message our page on TikTok or Facebook, or reach us on our contact page.
